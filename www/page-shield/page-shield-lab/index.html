01

CSP header probe

Verify whether Page Shield content security rules are active on this zone and identify the deployed mode.

Live edge response test

Re-fetches this page and inspects the HTTP response headers that Page Shield injects when a content security policy is configured: Content-Security-Policy-Report-Only (log mode) and Content-Security-Policy (enforce mode).

If no CSP headers come back, the zone either has no policy configured yet (plan-gated feature) or it is in the process of learning the baseline. The dashboard inventory of scripts, connections and cookies works separately and should still list this page's resources after a few minutes (connections/cookies require Business+).

        new script in your dashboard inventory — exactly what a real supply-chain compromise looks like.

        06
        

          
CSP log vs enforce modes

          
Choose report-only while tuning the allowlist; move to enforcement only after violations are understood.

        

      

      

      

        
ModeHTTP headerEffect

        
LogContent-Security-Policy-Report-OnlyScripts still run; the browser just reports violations to Cloudflare. Zero breakage — the safe starting point.

        
EnforceContent-Security-PolicyBrowser blocks anything not in the allowlist before it executes. Positive protection after the baseline is verified.

      

      
Best practice: monitor → log policy → review violation reports → enforce. Never jump straight to enforce.
